# KFL Aviation DMS — User Training Programme (90 minutes)

Audience: all DMS users (every role). Duration: 1 hour 30 minutes. Facilitator: named pilot facilitator. Format: instructor-led, deck-driven, with live demonstration in staging.

Materials: three slide decks (in `decks/`), the controlled manuals (in `manuals/`), and the reference documents (in `documents/`). This programme does not authorize staging access, invites, commits, or deployment.

## Session detail

### 1. Welcome, objectives and pilot scope (5 min)
State the purpose: train users on the DMS ahead of the restricted pilot. Emphasize that the pilot does not authorize staging access, deployment, source-PDF upload through the UI, commit, or account invitation without separate authorization. Walk the pilot scope (in scope / out of scope).

### 2. Accounts, roles, sign-in and session safety (10 min)
Demonstrate sign-in, the welcome screen, role switching, profile, change password, and notifications. Stress session safety: unique password, no sharing, lock screen, and that password change revokes other sessions. Reference the User Onboarding Procedure.

### 3. Finding and reading controlled manuals (15 min)
Demonstrate the document catalogue (search, filter), the document viewer (page rendering, outline navigation), and version comparison. Open a real controlled manual (Operations, Quality, Safety, Security, or Dangerous Goods) live. Stress that manuals are read-only and must not be reformatted.

### 4. Role workspaces (15 min)
Break out by role. Each lead walks its workspace: librarian (catalogue, distributions, change requests), quality (audits, findings, compliance, controller dashboard), safety (occurrences, investigations, hazards, actions, security records), crew/training (crew, qualifications, duty time, licences, renewals), maintenance/operations (maintenance, aircraft insurance, ROC, missions, DG, self-service upload), auditor (audit trail, audit reports, provenance import).

### 5. Compliance workflows (15 min)
Walk the end-to-end flows: raise and process a change request; run a quality audit and record findings; report an occurrence and track an investigation and safety action; manage dangerous goods shipments. Include the provenance import demonstration (stage, review, verify, reject) for the auditor and quality roles.

### 6. Administration, backup and restore (10 min)
For system administrators and IT: service topology, deployment model, backup scope and schedule, restore procedure, off-host restore target, and the admin observability page. Reference the Onboarding Runbook, Production Deployment, and Disaster Recovery documents.

### 7. Incident response and escalation (10 min)
Walk incident categories, the response steps (detect, contain, notify, resolve, document), the do-not list, and the escalation contacts. Confirm maintenance windows.

### 8. Q&A, action items and go/no-go (10 min)
Capture questions, assign action items from the participant workbook, and set the go/no-go date. Re-state that passing this training enables the pilot; operational readiness requires Accountable Manager sign-off after open items close.
